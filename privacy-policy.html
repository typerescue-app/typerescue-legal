Type Rescue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Publisher: Type Rescue · Contact: typerescue.app@gmail.com

What Type Rescue does

Type Rescue is a Chrome extension for recovering text entered into supported website fields. Tracking is off until you turn it on. When tracking is on, the extension observes supported editable fields on pages where Chrome grants it access and saves recoverable text in this Chrome profile.

Information stored on your device

Depending on the field and website, saved records may include the text you typed (including email addresses or other personal information), a field label, basic field attributes used to identify and restore that field, the website host and page/frame path, and save timestamps. Premium history may also retain earlier non-empty versions.

Drafts and preferences are stored with Chrome's local extension storage. Type Rescue does not encrypt this local data, and it is not sent to Type Rescue's licensing service. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or Chrome profile may be able to access it. The extension does not provide cross-device draft synchronization.

You can turn tracking off and delete fields or saved tabs, including using Delete All. At the saved-tab limit, the oldest record may be replaced after you acknowledge the replacement policy. Browser-profile backups or other software may retain copies outside Type Rescue's control; uninstalling the extension may not erase every such copy.

Sensitive information

Password and other sensitive-field detection is best-effort and can miss content, including when a website changes how a field works. Do not use Type Rescue to save passwords, PINs, payment-card information, authentication codes, or other secrets. Review saved text before copying or restoring it.

Purchase and licensing information

If you start checkout or use Restore Purchase, the licensing service processes information needed to create and verify a purchase or license. This can include your purchase email, purchase and payment-link identifiers, amount and currency, payment status, purchase and expiry timestamps, restore-code hashes, and a signed entitlement token. Type Rescue does not receive or store full card credentials; Razorpay hosts checkout and handles payment details under its own policies.

The licensing service is designed to use Cloudflare Workers and D1. Restore codes are sent through Resend. Cloudflare and Resend may process service and request metadata to operate and protect their services. The backend uses derived, hashed request identifiers for rate limiting; those identifiers are not the raw network address. Captured webpage draft text is not sent to these providers by Type Rescue.

Premium is a one-time ₹399 purchase for three years, with no subscription or automatic renewal. Licensing and transaction records are retained as needed to operate and restore licenses, prevent abuse, respond to disputes or refunds, and meet applicable legal or accounting obligations. Restore codes are intended to expire after ten minutes. The service does not currently publish a fixed deletion date for transaction records or a guaranteed cleanup schedule for expired code records.

How information is used and shared

Local drafts are used to provide capture, history, copy, and restore features. Purchase information is used to create checkout, verify payment, activate or restore a license, process refund or revocation events, prevent abuse, and answer support requests. We do not sell captured draft text or use it for advertising.

Information is shared with service providers only as needed for their roles: Razorpay for hosted checkout and payments, Cloudflare for the licensing API and database, and Resend for restore-code email. Their own privacy terms also apply to information they process.

Security

Licensing requests use HTTPS. The backend verifies payment notifications before activating Premium and keeps payment-signing credentials on the server. These measures do not make local drafts encrypted and cannot guarantee that every security risk is eliminated. Keep your device and Chrome profile protected, and avoid saving sensitive information.

Your requests and contact

You can delete local records from the extension. For questions or a request about purchase/licensing records, email typerescue.app@gmail.com from the purchase email where possible and include the purchase reference if available. Do not email passwords, payment-card details, PINs, or restore codes. We may need to verify a request and may retain records required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute purposes.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the product or its data practices change. The current effective date appears at the top. Material changes to tracking or data use will be disclosed in the extension before the changed practice is enabled.
