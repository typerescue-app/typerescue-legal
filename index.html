Your drafts remain in your Chrome profile

Type Rescue stores captured drafts locally in Chrome. Drafts are not uploaded to the licensing service. Local drafts are not encrypted, and sensitive-field filtering is best-effort.

Avoid capturing passwords, PINs, payment-card details, authentication codes, or other secrets. Use tracking only on fields you want saved.

Good to know

Clear expectations make recovery easier.

Does Type Rescue submit a form?

No. Restore places text into a matching field where supported. You review and submit the page yourself.

Will it work on every website?

Capture and restore depend on the page, the field, and Chrome access. Some editors and sites may not be supported.

How does Premium activate?

After Razorpay checkout, the licensing service verifies payment and activates Premium in the Chrome profile that started checkout.

Is Premium recurring?

No. Premium is a one-time ₹399 payment for three years, with no subscription and no automatic renewal.
