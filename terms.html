Using Type Rescue

Type Rescue helps recover text entered into supported webpage fields. You choose whether tracking is enabled. When enabled, capture depends on Chrome permissions, the page, and the extension’s field detection. Type Rescue does not submit forms for you. Some sites or editors may not be supported, and no uninterrupted or error-free operation is promised.

You are responsible for deciding what to capture and for reviewing text before copying or restoring it. Sensitive-field detection is best-effort and may miss information. Do not rely on Type Rescue to protect passwords, PINs, payment-card details, authentication codes, or other secrets. See the Privacy Policy for data practices.
